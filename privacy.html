Privacy Policy

AtmosphereNetwork Uploader · last updated 13 November 2026

Who uses this app

AtmosphereNetwork Uploader is a private tool operated by AMT Studios. It is used only to publish AMT Studios' own videos to AMT Studios' own YouTube channels. It is not available to the public.

What Google data it uses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API with these permissions:

  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos.
  • Manage YouTube videos and captions (youtube.force-ssl): to set each video's thumbnail and captions, and to read the channel's own ID to confirm the upload goes to the right channel.

The app does not read, collect or store viewers' data, comments, subscriber lists, analytics or any other person's information.

How the data is stored

The permission tokens Google issues are stored only on AMT Studios' own computer, in a folder that only the owner can read. They are never sold, shared with anyone, sent to any other service, or used for advertising or to train AI models.

How to remove access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's Third-party apps & services page. Removing it stops the app immediately.

Google API Services User Data Policy

AtmosphereNetwork Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
